How v2 works The default skill is built from a study of 247 curated sites. A build runs in six steps: Reads the brief and lists what only this client has. A script deals three of 20 researched directions and the agent picks one. Finds the idea: one sentence tying the client's own material to that direction. Builds with real pictures and footage from open collections, credited. Checks the page in a real browser at three widths and fails it on slop. Looks at its own screenshots and fixes the top three problems, up to three rounds. A build takes roughly 30 to 45 minutes and a few hundred thousand tokens. Its direction comes from the draw and its limits from the inspector. Common Questions What does the default skill need? Node 22 or newer and Chrome, Edge or Chromium. It installs nothing and uses no API keys. It needs the internet only to find pictures. Where do the pictures come from, and can I use them? From open collections such as Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and the Met. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA files are offered, and the skill puts the credits on the page. Replace them with your own when you have them. Does it work with React, Vue, Svelte, plain HTML? Yes. The rules are about design, not a framework. Is it for dashboards and app screens? No. The default skill is for public-facing pages. Use minimalist-skill or soft-skill for product UI.
