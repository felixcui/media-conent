## 摘要

## 正文

I asked @jxnlco (DevEx at OpenAI): “What’s the craziest thing Codex did for you?”

Here’s his answer:

“I was on a bike ride when a coworker asked me to fix a launch video. So I connected remotely from my phone and asked Codex to use computer use to edit the video, export it, and post it back to Slack.

Twenty minutes later, there was a new video in the Slack thread. Then I told Codex:

‘Every 30 minutes, check the thread for feedback and export V2, V3, V4.’

By the time I got home, the launch video was greenlit.”

Codex had handled the revisions by repeatedly checking Slack for feedback.
